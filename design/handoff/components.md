# Clove MVP: component inventory

These are the reusable pieces behind every screen. Sizes are in points, measured on the iPhone frames (390 × 844) unless noted. Token names refer to `tokens/` (code name `Color.clove.<token>`). Icons are SF Symbols, and text is SF Pro at the system text styles. Use Dynamic Type styles rather than the fixed sizes in Figma. Timings (toast auto-hide, chip fade) and SF Symbol names are **proposed** defaults, not reviewed design decisions.

Figma note: the frames still contain some **hidden legacy layers**, named "… · local (hidden)", "… · source …" or "Segmented / Toolbar". They are left over from the original concept. Only build what is visible in a screenshot of the node.

## Shared glass style

All floating controls use one glass material:
- Fill `surface/glass`, a background blur of about 36, and a soft drop shadow (about 16 blur).
- On iOS 26 and later, use the system Liquid Glass (`.glassEffect()`), with `surface/glass` as the fallback. Never put glass on the photo itself, only over it.

## Grid

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Photo tile** | Square, aspect fill. iPhone: 3 columns of 129 with about 1.5 gaps. Duo cover: 3 columns. Duo expanded: 6 columns (143) in Grid View, 2 columns in Photo Preview. iPad: adaptive, about 6 columns in Grid View, 2 columns in Photo Preview. Overlays: a rating badge (bottom left, a small dark pill with "★3") and a label dot (bottom right, 8 pt, `label/*`). |
| **Rejected tile** | The same tile at `opacity/rejected`. No flag badge. |
| **Group stack** | Shown whenever Similar groups is On (`visibleMembers.count >= 2`). Uses the group's **Representative Photo** cover with 1–2 offset peeking cards drawn only from remaining `visibleMembers`, and a top-right count badge (`visibleMembers.count`): a compact rounded rectangle (radius 4, padding 6/2) filled with black at 56%, white text at 94% in system Medium 11 pt. Dimmed (`opacity/rejected`) only when all `visibleMembers` are rejected. No outline and no "Burst" tag. Groups with `visibleMembers.count == 1` render as standalone tiles. |
| **Expanded group surface** | The grid's inline Select-mode expanded group tray and Photo Preview split grid's group run draw a neutral surface behind their members: `text/primary` at 12% layer opacity on the background shape alone, radius 6 per tile (owner decision, 2026-11-13, #226, #227). The opacity never tints photo pixels, and the active member uses the full-opacity `accent` current-photo indicator (`1.5 pt` inset outline + centered `14 × 2 pt` bottom locator, radius 6). |
| **Inline group tray** (select mode) | Continuous horizontal tray band behind unfolded active group members (`>= 2` visible members): `text/primary` at 12% fill with a 1 pt `text/primary` 20% border along top, bottom, and outer caps (omitting internal vertical dividers), `8 pt` outer corners, `0 pt` connected interior corners across same-row neighbors, and a trailing `1.5 pt` gap bridge. Inner photo thumbnails use `4 pt` top/bottom/outer and `2 pt` connected-edge insets clipped to `6 pt` continuous radius. The first member (`memberIndex == 0`) carries a decorative non-interactive `square.stack` + count pill at top leading (`allowsHitTesting(false)`, `accessibilityHidden(true)`), and each member adds `"Group {i} of {n}"` to its tile accessibility label. |
| **Group run** (Photo Preview split) | Driven by `browsing.expandedGroup`: when nil, the split grid draws the whole filtered folder with its multi-photo visible groups collapsed as stacks (highlighting the stack holding `currentPhoto`). When set, the pane draws only that group's visible members — the same isolated content as the Focus View filmstrip — on the **expanded group surface** with a 30 pt header reading "{n} photos" and a `chevron.up` fold control (44 × 30) at its trailing edge. Members show no count badge; no other photo or group is drawn. |
| **No-preview tile** | `surface/card` fill, the file extension ("CR3") in `text/secondary` semibold, and "No preview" below it. |
| **Placeholder tile** (opening) | A plain `surface/card` square, no shimmer. |
| **Selection check** | The standard iOS blue checkmark circle at the bottom right of a selected tile. Used only in select mode. |

## Detail and filmstrip

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Photo stage** | The photo is aspect fit on `bg/stage`. It is never tinted or dimmed, including when rejected. Double-tap zooms to 100%. |
| **Detail reject glyph** | A white `flag.slash.fill` in a 22 pt filled `reject` circle, inset 8 pt in the stage's top-trailing corner inside the safe area (owner decision, 2026-11-13, #227; it was an `xmark`). Shown only for rejected photos. Fixed while zooming or panning and visible with chrome hidden. Decorative and ignores taps. VoiceOver reads "Rejected" in the photo label, with no separate glyph element. |
| **Nav title and subtitle** | The filename (or folder name), with a subtitle built from counts and companion segments joined by " · ": `"{i} / {n}"` across Timeline slots in Timeline mode, or `"Group · {i} / {n}"` in Group Focus mode. Only folder access problems add an indicator: the `externaldrive.badge.xmark` or `lock.fill` glyph in `text/secondary` after the count, with "Not connected" or "View only" as its VoiceOver label. |
| **Current-photo indicator** | Marks the single settled current photo (or the collapsed stack holding it) across Grid, Split Photo Preview (standalone tiles at radius `0`, collapsed stacks anchored to the front cover card at radius `5`, and Group Focus members at radius `6`), and Group Photo Grid Picker (radius `6`). Draws a `1.5 pt` inset outline and a centered `14 × 2 pt` bottom locator (along the inner bottom edge at `1.5 pt` bottom inset) in the theme's `accent` colour, with no fill, glow, shadow, photo tint, or change to tile dimensions or spacing. Placed at full opacity after rejected thumbnail dimming, decorative only (`allowsHitTesting(false)`, `accessibilityHidden(true)`), while keeping `.isSelected` on the parent tile. |
| **Filmstrip current-photo marker** | Marks the current item across Detail / Focus View filmstrip (standalone tile, collapsed stack containing the current photo, or Group Focus member) and Compare's challenger filmstrip (strictly pinned to challenger **B**). Raises the current thumbnail or collapsed stack `6 pt` relative to neighbors and centers an opaque `12 × 4 pt` rounded capsule (`Capsule()`) `2 pt` below the thumbnail or front cover card (`text/primary` core fill with a `1 pt` `bg/canvas` inside edge via `strokeBorder`, so Light renders a dark core with a light edge and Dark renders a light core with a dark edge). Always drawn at full opacity (`1.0`), even when the rejected thumbnail or stack above it is dimmed, decorative and non-interactive (`allowsHitTesting(false)`, `accessibilityHidden(true)`), while preserving `.isSelected` on the parent tile and keeping thumbnail dimensions, horizontal spacing, and band height constant. |
| **Filmstrip** | Thumbnails `54 × 60` inside a constant `76 pt` standard band (`48 × 48` inside `62 pt` in compact iPhone landscape), shown in Focus View (hidden in Photo Preview), using a unified `3 pt` horizontal thumbnail spacing across Timeline (standard and compact) and Group Focus. Operates at two explicit levels as two independent tracks so **Timeline** keeps its `ScrollView` alive across Group Focus without resetting offset or shifting neighbors (#304, #318): **Timeline** (`expandedGroup == nil`, gap `3`) renders standalone photos and collapsed multi-photo group stacks in grid order with no inline expansion (the current photo or stack raises `6 pt` with the `12 × 4 pt` two-tone marker `2 pt` below its thumbnail or front cover card, and stacks show their Representative Photo star mark); tapping the current stack or dragging downward (`>= 24 pt`) on any stack enters Group Focus. **Group Focus** (`expandedGroup != nil`, gap `3`) floats with the exact same background-free transparent presentation as Timeline (no background tray wrapper), rendering **only** the expanded group's visible members (`1 … N`), hugging natural content width on short groups and framing them with `36 × 36 pt` native Liquid Glass circular buttons (`.cloveGlass(in: Circle())`, `text/primary` glyph, vertically centered against the non-current thumbnail height beside the strip): a trailing `chevron.up` collapse button (`clove.detail.filmstrip.group.fold`, always visible) and a leading `square.grid.2x2` grid picker button (`clove.detail.filmstrip.group.gridPicker`, shown **only when `visibleMembers` overflow the current outer filmstrip width**), while the current member raises `6 pt` with the two-tone marker `2 pt` below it; dragging upward (`>= 24 pt`) anywhere on the strip collapses back to Timeline. Across both Timeline and overflowing Group Focus, the active item stays centered in its visible scroll viewport (naturally clamping at head/tail without half-viewport padding, and keeping short non-overflowing groups centered without scrolling): centering is instant on initial appearance, entering Group Focus, switching groups, collapsing back to Timeline, and metrics/size-class/rotation changes, and smoothly animated (`CloveMotion.chrome`, instant under Reduce Motion) on selection changes, while manual filmstrip dragging browses freely without snapping back until `current` changes. Level transitions (~240 ms damped spring; crossfade under Reduce Motion) roll both tracks vertically with subtle X-axis 3D drum tilt (`24°`) and fan members horizontally on expand or gather inward on collapse with a subtle settle pulse and selection haptic. Rejected thumbnails are dimmed while the two-tone marker remains at full `1.0` opacity. The active pill (`Expand {n} photos` or `Reject {n} unmarked`) floats centred above the strip, drawn by the detail chrome and never by the strip's own height (#305). |
| **Group photo grid picker** | Adaptive 3-column thumbnail grid (`4 pt` grid spacing, `12 pt` padding) opened from the overflow-gated leading `square.grid.2x2` button in Group Focus. Presents as a `.sheet` on compact widths (filling the sheet with ~115–125 pt square tiles and a `Done` toolbar button) or a `444 pt` wide `.popover` anchored to the grid picker button on regular widths (up to `520 pt` tall on regular vertical height and `280 pt` on compact height, ~137 pt square tiles). Retains `CurrentPhotoIndicator(cornerRadius: 6)`, rating badge, color label dot, `rejectedOpacity`, auto-scroll to `current`, tap-to-select & dismiss, and auto-dismiss when collapsing, switching groups, changing posture/size class, or when the group stops overflowing the strip. |
| **100% chip** | A small glass pill reading "100%", fading out after about 1 s. |
| **Info sheet** | System sheet at the medium detent, with grouped list sections Capture and File. |
| **Focus regions toggle** | Native toolbar `viewfinder` button (~44 pt hit target) next to Back on iPhone/iPad and Duo half fold's top bar, floating in a glass circle beside the upper-leading viewport photo identity badge in expanded Duo (Photo Preview and Focus View, #390), and in Duo cover's right action rail. Toggles AF, face, and eye overlays and their hit/accessibility targets together for the current session (default On, unpersisted). `accent` when On, neutral `text/primary` when Off; accessibility label "Focus regions", value "On"/"Off". |
| **Focus overlays** | Camera-style no-fill outlines in `focus/outline` with `focus/edge` contrast edge (`1.25 pt` normal, `1.5 pt` `accent` when selected for 100% inspection; subdued dashed when unavailable): closed rectangle for camera AF, four corner L-brackets for face fallback, and small centred square (`16 pt` screen-space actionable, `12 pt` unavailable) for the single selected eye per person. Screen-space stroke widths, eye square size, and >= 44 pt hit targets stay invariant through zoom without inflating visible markers. Local AF collisions suppress only the conflicting subject's analysis marker; separated subjects and AF coexist. |
| **Focus result indicators** | Passive `16 pt` glyphs in a compact horizontal row in the photo viewport's upper-leading corner, below the header (or below the top-leading viewport overlay row on expanded Duo) and inside its safe area (above any `Preview only` note; in Photo Preview split, inside the 70% photo pane): `person.fill.viewfinder` when an actual face or eye result exists (even if its overlay is suppressed by a camera AF rectangle), `person.slash` only when a completed analysis explicitly reports no faces, and a separate slashed `viewfinder` only when camera AF metadata is explicitly missing. Available AF regions continue to render through the stage overlay without a corner badge. Uses a subtle dark scrim (`focus/edge` pill with `focus/outline` glyphs) only where the icons cover photo pixels; otherwise draws in `text/primary` without backing. Hides when Focus regions is Off or chrome is hidden, stays silent during pending, cancelled, or failed paths, clears immediately on photo change, ignores stale results, and ignores taps. Each visible glyph has a localized VoiceOver label (`"Face detected"`, `"No faces detected"`, `"No autofocus metadata"`). |
| **Compare workspace** | Side-by-side panes `A` (reference) and `B` (challenger) inside rounded containers (`accent` `2 pt` active border, `separator` `1 pt` inactive border), each with a slim header (`A`/`B` pill, extension-preserving filename, zoom readout, and read-only marks summary). Top bar holds `Done`, `Compare`, and `Sync On` / `Sync Off`. Bottom review bar anchors `[Undo] [A/B badge] [Marking Capsule]` trailing and (`stepping` mode only) `<`, `>`, `Set as A` plus compact `A`/`B` filmstrip (`44 × 44` tiles with `3 pt` spacing inside a `56 pt` strip; challenger `B` stays raised `6 pt` with the `12 × 4 pt` two-tone marker `2 pt` below regardless of `activeSide`, while `A`/`B` badge emphasis tracks `activeSide`; `B` remains centered in the strip viewport with natural head/tail edge clamping — instant on initial appearance and layout/rotation changes, `CloveMotion.chrome` animated on challenger changes, and free manual drag scrolling without snapping back until `B` changes) on iPad & unfolded Duo. |

## Feedback

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Progress line** | 2 pt high, full width under the toolbar. Fills in `accent` while reading and in `group` while grouping. Hidden when done. |
| **Toast** | A glass pill, 358 × 55, radius 26, padding 18/8, gap 12. Text on the left (with an optional second line in `reject`) and a button on the right (Undo or Details, a 72 × 38 capsule in `accent`). Sits above the capsule or the Move Rejects button. Auto-hides after about 6 s; Undo stays available in the top bar. **Failure toast** variant: one line, no button: "Couldn't save the mark." or "Couldn't read some marks." In detail it sits above the filmstrip. |
| **Banner** (errors) | A glass card pinned under the toolbar, with the message, then one button (Reopen or Open). The grid behind it is dimmed. |
| **Alert** | A system alert. The destructive option is red, and the safe option is bold. |
| **Pill action** (detail) | A glass capsule floating centred above the filmstrip without taking any of its layout height, so the stage never resizes (or at the bottom of the stage in Photo Preview): **"Expand {n} photos"** (text in `text/primary`) on an unexpanded multi-photo stack in Timeline, pulsing subtly on vertical stage drags when marking is disabled; or **"Reject {n} unmarked"** (text in `reject`) in Group Focus when the visible group has both a rated or labeled member and an unmarked member. Hides with chrome; `"Reject {n} unmarked"` also hides when marking is off or no unmarked photos remain. |
| **Move Rejects sheet** | A small detent. A fanned stack of 3–4 reject thumbnails (tap to expand into a 4-column quick grid), the title, a one-line explanation, and a full-width **Move** button in `accent`. |
| **Couldn't move sheet** | A list sheet with rows of a thumbnail, file name and reason (in `reject`), then one full-width **Done**. No Try again. |
| **Folder preparation indicator** | Plain 14 pt progress ring beside the folder title (Grid / iPad sidebar) and filename (Detail across iPhone, iPad, Duo cover, and half fold), with no glass base, filled circle, border, or shadow, and an ~44 × 44 pt invisible hit target (#293). On expanded Duo Photo Preview and Focus View, the same 14 pt ring floats beside the upper-leading viewport photo identity badge inside a native circular glass backdrop (`>= 44 × 44` pt hit target) so it stays legible over both bright/dark photographs and the stage (#390). `accent` arc over subtle `text/secondary` track driven by whole-folder readiness (`readyForBothCount / totalPhotos`). Shows an indeterminate small `ProgressView` when total count is unknown, a static muted `pause.fill` ring when waiting/paused without implying active progress, `exclamationmark.circle` in `status/failed` on material issues, and briefly a `checkmark` ring on completion before withdrawing. |
| **Folder preparation details** | Adaptive native `.popover` anchored directly to the inline indicator on regular widths (iPad, Duo expanded), or native `.sheet` (medium/large detents with **Done**) on compact widths (iPhone, Duo cover). Identifies the folder, reports `{ready} of {total} ready` (intersection of analysis-complete and preview-prepared identities), shows two separate linear `ProgressView` bars (**Focus analysis** and **Previews**) with honest counts, surfaces waiting/paused state and material issues without adding Pause or Cancel controls. |
