# Clove MVP: component inventory

These are the reusable pieces behind every screen. Sizes are in points, measured on the iPhone frames (390 × 844) unless noted. Token names refer to `tokens/` (code name `Color.clove.<token>`). Icons are SF Symbols, and text is SF Pro at the system text styles. Use Dynamic Type styles rather than the fixed sizes in Figma. Timings (toast auto-hide, chip fade) and SF Symbol names are **proposed** defaults, not reviewed design decisions.

Figma note: the frames still contain some **hidden legacy layers**, named "… · local (hidden)", "… · source …" or "Segmented / Toolbar". They are left over from the original concept. Only build what is visible in a screenshot of the node.

## Shared glass style

All floating controls use one glass material:
- Fill `surface/glass`, a background blur of about 36, and a soft drop shadow (about 16 blur).
- On iOS 26 and later, use the system Liquid Glass (`.glassEffect()`), with `surface/glass` as the fallback. Never put glass on the photo itself, only over it.

The **clear stage** shows `star.slash` in `text/secondary` with all stars empty. All five levels always show; disabled levels are dimmed and are not input. The display-only swipe indicator appears beside the touch start (24 pt to the left of the finger, or to its right when the touch starts in the left third) and does not move during the swipe. iPad rail version: 43 × 174, radius 22, padding 10, gap 6, tap or drag, no chip. |

## Grid

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Photo tile** | Square, aspect fill. iPhone: 3 columns of 129 with about 1.5 gaps. Duo cover: 3 columns. Duo expanded: 6 columns (143) in Grid View, 2 columns in Photo Preview. iPad: adaptive, about 6 columns in Grid View, 2 columns in Photo Preview. Overlays: a rating badge (bottom left, a small dark pill with "★3") and a label dot (bottom right, 8 pt, `label/*`). |
| **Rejected tile** | The same tile at `opacity/rejected`. No flag badge. |
| **Group stack** | Shown whenever Similar groups is On (`visibleMembers.count >= 2`). Uses the group's **Representative Photo** cover with 1–2 offset peeking cards drawn only from remaining `visibleMembers`, and a top-right count badge (`visibleMembers.count`): a compact rounded rectangle (radius 4, padding 6/2) filled with black at 56%, white text at 94% in system Medium 11 pt. Dimmed (`opacity/rejected`) only when all `visibleMembers` are rejected. No outline and no "Burst" tag. Groups with `visibleMembers.count == 1` render as standalone tiles. |
| **Expanded group surface** | The grid's expanded group members sit on the same neutral surface as the filmstrip's group extent: `text/primary` at 12% layer opacity on the background shape alone, radius 6 per tile (owner decision, 2026-11-13, #226 filmstrip, #227 grid). The opacity never tints photo pixels, and the current-photo ring stays the solid `text/primary` stroke. |
| **Group run** (Photo Preview split) | Driven by `browsing.expandedGroup`: when nil, the split grid draws the whole filtered folder with its multi-photo visible groups collapsed as stacks (highlighting the stack holding `currentPhoto`). When set, the pane draws only that group's visible members — the same isolated content as the Focus View filmstrip — on the **expanded group surface** with a 30 pt header reading "{n} photos" and a `chevron.up` fold control (44 × 30) at its trailing edge. Members show no count badge; no other photo or group is drawn. |
| **No-preview tile** | `surface/card` fill, the file extension ("CR3") in `text/secondary` semibold, and "No preview" below it. |
| **Placeholder tile** (opening) | A plain `surface/card` square, no shimmer. |
| **Selection check** | The standard iOS blue checkmark circle at the bottom right of a selected tile. Used only in select mode. |

## Detail and filmstrip

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Photo stage** | The photo is aspect fit on `bg/stage`. It is never tinted or dimmed, including when rejected. Double-tap zooms to 100%. |
| **Detail reject glyph** | A white `flag.slash.fill` in a 22 pt filled `reject` circle, inset 8 pt in the stage's top-trailing corner inside the safe area (owner decision, 2026-11-13, #227; it was an `xmark`). Shown only for rejected photos. Fixed while zooming or panning and visible with chrome hidden. Decorative and ignores taps. VoiceOver reads "Rejected" in the photo label, with no separate glyph element. |
| **Nav title and subtitle** | The filename (or folder name), with a subtitle built from counts and companion segments joined by " · ": `"{i} / {n}"` across Timeline slots in Timeline mode, or `"Group · {i} / {n}"` in Group Focus mode. Only folder access problems add an indicator: the `externaldrive.badge.xmark` or `lock.fill` glyph in `text/secondary` after the count, with "Not connected" or "View only" as its VoiceOver label. |
| **Filmstrip** | Thumbnails 54 × 60 inside a constant `70 pt` band, shown in Focus View (hidden in Photo Preview). Operates at two explicit levels as two independent tracks so **Timeline** keeps its `ScrollView` alive across Group Focus without resetting offset or shifting neighbors (#304, #318): **Timeline** (`expandedGroup == nil`, gap 8) renders standalone photos and collapsed multi-photo group stacks in grid order with no inline expansion (current stack shows its Representative Photo ring and star mark); tapping the current stack or dragging downward (`>= 24 pt`) on any stack enters Group Focus. **Group Focus** (`expandedGroup != nil`, gap 3, padding 3, radius 9) renders **only** the expanded group's visible members (`1 … N`) inside `ExpandedGroupSurface` (`text/primary` at 12% layer opacity), hugging natural content width on short groups and pinning a non-scrolling trailing `chevron.up` collapse cap (`30 × 60`, accessibility label `"Collapse group"`, `clove.detail.filmstrip.group.fold`) on the right; dragging upward (`>= 24 pt`) anywhere on the strip collapses back to Timeline. Level transitions (~240 ms damped spring; crossfade under Reduce Motion) roll both tracks vertically with subtle X-axis 3D drum tilt (`24°`) and fan members horizontally onto `ExpandedGroupSurface` on expand or gather inward on collapse with a subtle settle pulse and selection haptic. Rejected thumbnails are dimmed. The active pill (`Expand {n} photos` or `Reject {n} unmarked`) floats centred above the strip, drawn by the detail chrome and never by the strip's own height (#305). |
| **100% chip** | A small glass pill reading "100%", fading out after about 1 s. |
| **Info sheet** | System sheet at the medium detent, with grouped list sections Capture and File. |
| **Focus regions toggle** | Native toolbar `viewfinder` button (~44 pt hit target) next to Back on iPhone/iPad and Duo half fold's top bar, at the leading edge of the photo stage header in expanded Duo (Photo Preview and Focus View), and in Duo cover's right action rail. Toggles AF, face, and eye overlays and their hit/accessibility targets together for the current session (default On, unpersisted). `accent` when On, neutral `text/primary` when Off; accessibility label "Focus regions", value "On"/"Off". |
| **Focus overlays** | Camera-style no-fill outlines in `focus/outline` with `focus/edge` contrast edge (`1.25 pt` normal, `1.5 pt` `accent` when selected for 100% inspection; subdued dashed when unavailable): closed rectangle for camera AF, four corner L-brackets for face fallback, and small centred square (`16 pt` screen-space actionable, `12 pt` unavailable) for the single selected eye per person. Screen-space stroke widths, eye square size, and >= 44 pt hit targets stay invariant through zoom without inflating visible markers. Local AF collisions suppress only the conflicting subject's analysis marker; separated subjects and AF coexist. |
| **Focus result indicators** | Passive `16 pt` glyphs in a compact horizontal row in the photo viewport's upper-leading corner, below the header and inside its safe area (above any `Preview only` note; in Photo Preview split, inside the 70% photo pane): `person.fill.viewfinder` when an actual face or eye result exists (even if its overlay is suppressed by a camera AF rectangle), `person.slash` only when a completed analysis explicitly reports no faces, and a separate slashed `viewfinder` only when camera AF metadata is explicitly missing. Available AF regions continue to render through the stage overlay without a corner badge. Uses a subtle dark scrim (`focus/edge` pill with `focus/outline` glyphs) only where the icons cover photo pixels; otherwise draws in `text/primary` without backing. Hides when Focus regions is Off or chrome is hidden, stays silent during pending, cancelled, or failed paths, clears immediately on photo change, ignores stale results, and ignores taps. Each visible glyph has a localized VoiceOver label (`"Face detected"`, `"No faces detected"`, `"No autofocus metadata"`). |

## Feedback

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Progress line** | 2 pt high, full width under the toolbar. Fills in `accent` while reading and in `group` while grouping. Hidden when done. |
| **Toast** | A glass pill, 358 × 55, radius 26, padding 18/8, gap 12. Text on the left (with an optional second line in `reject`) and a button on the right (Undo or Details, a 72 × 38 capsule in `accent`). Sits above the capsule or the Move Rejects button. Auto-hides after about 6 s; Undo stays available in the top bar. **Failure toast** variant: one line, no button: "Couldn't save the mark." or "Couldn't read some marks." In detail it sits above the filmstrip. |
| **Banner** (errors) | A glass card pinned under the toolbar, with the message, then one button (Reopen or Open). The grid behind it is dimmed. |
| **Alert** | A system alert. The destructive option is red, and the safe option is bold. |
| **Pill action** (detail) | A glass capsule floating centred above the filmstrip without taking any of its layout height, so the stage never resizes (or at the bottom of the stage in Photo Preview): **"Expand {n} photos"** (text in `text/primary`) on an unexpanded multi-photo stack in Timeline, pulsing subtly on vertical stage drags when marking is disabled; or **"Reject {n} unmarked"** (text in `reject`) in Group Focus when the visible group has both a rated or labeled member and an unmarked member. Hides with chrome; `"Reject {n} unmarked"` also hides when marking is off or no unmarked photos remain. |
| **Move Rejects sheet** | A small detent. A fanned stack of 3–4 reject thumbnails (tap to expand into a 4-column quick grid), the title, a one-line explanation, and a full-width **Move** button in `accent`. |
| **Couldn't move sheet** | A list sheet with rows of a thumbnail, file name and reason (in `reject`), then one full-width **Done**. No Try again. |
| **Folder preparation indicator** | Plain 14 pt progress ring beside the folder title (Grid / iPad sidebar) and filename (Detail across iPhone, iPad, Duo cover, Duo expanded photo header, and half fold), with no glass base, filled circle, border, or shadow, and an ~44 × 44 pt invisible hit target (#293). `accent` arc over subtle `text/secondary` track driven by whole-folder readiness (`readyForBothCount / totalPhotos`). Shows an indeterminate small `ProgressView` when total count is unknown, a static muted `pause.fill` ring when waiting/paused without implying active progress, `exclamationmark.circle` in `status/failed` on material issues, and briefly a `checkmark` ring on completion before withdrawing. |
| **Folder preparation details** | Adaptive native `.popover` anchored directly to the inline indicator on regular widths (iPad, Duo expanded), or native `.sheet` (medium/large detents with **Done**) on compact widths (iPhone, Duo cover). Identifies the folder, reports `{ready} of {total} ready` (intersection of analysis-complete and preview-prepared identities), shows two separate linear `ProgressView` bars (**Focus analysis** and **Previews**) with honest counts, surfaces waiting/paused state and material issues without adding Pause or Cancel controls. |

## Icons (SF Symbols)

`chevron.left` (Back), `sidebar.left` (Photo Preview / Focus View toggle), `viewfinder` (Focus regions toggle; overlaid with a diagonal slash for missing AF metadata), `person.fill.viewfinder` / `person.slash` (Focus face result indicators), `line.3.horizontal.decrease` (Filter), `ellipsis` ("…"), `info.circle` (Info), `arrow.uturn.backward` (Undo), `tray.and.arrow.down` (Move Rejects), `flag.slash` / `flag.slash.fill` (Reject, owner decision 2026-11-13, #227; the old `xmark` is reserved for Close/Dismiss), `star` / `star.fill`, `star.slash` (Clear rating), `circle` / `circle.fill` (Label), `circle.slash` (Clear label), `gearshape` (Settings), `checkmark` / `checkmark.circle.fill` (Completion / Selection), `pause.fill` / `pause.circle` (Waiting/paused preparation), `exclamationmark.circle` (Preparation issues), `externaldrive.badge.xmark` (Disconnected).
