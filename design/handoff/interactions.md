# Clove MVP: interactions and flows

Items tagged **(proposed)** were not reviewed in the design. They are sensible defaults for the implementer; confirm them with the owner before treating them as final.

## Core model

- A **mark** is a rating and a label (none or one of 5 colors). The rating can be −1, which is Reject, so a photo is never rated and rejected at once: rejecting replaces the stars, and undoing restores them. Marks are written to the photo's XMP sidecar, in the folder where the photos live; a standalone JPEG/HIF keeps its rating and label in Clove. Nothing is imported or copied.
- Every mark appears at once and saves in user action order. A pending mark is shown until its write finishes; otherwise the files supply the marks. There is no save status. Only folder access problems appear in the subtitle, as a glyph (`externaldrive.badge.xmark` or `lock.fill`); "Not connected" and "View only" are its VoiceOver labels.
- **Undo** reverts the last mark change, a batch mark/keyword change, a "Reject N unmarked", or a Move Rejects. Detail has no permanent top-bar or dock Undo button; instead, `⌘Z` remains active on hardware keyboards and a transient ~4 s Liquid Glass Undo toast appears centered `12 pt` above the filmstrip in Focus View (or above the bottom bar in Photo Preview) only after `"Reject N unmarked"` or a mark that hides the current photo under an active live filter (marks that keep the photo visible show no toast). Compare likewise omits any permanent bottom-bar Undo button (keeping `⌘Z` hardware Undo active on the active pane), Grid keeps its `Move Rejects` Undo toast, and Expanded Duo Grid keeps its dock Undo. It is a stack and works across photos.
- **Reject never deletes.** Rejected photos stay in place (dimmed in the grid and filmstrip; full brightness with a corner glyph in detail) until the person runs Move Rejects, which moves them to `_Rejected/` inside the same folder.

## App flow

```
Entry ──tap hero card──▶ Grid (scrolled to last photo) ──▶ Detail at that photo (Timeline)
  │                       │
  └─Open folder─▶ picker ─┘ (opening: reading ▶ grouping, grid usable throughout)

Grid ─tap standalone photo─▶ Detail (Timeline, expandedGroup = nil)
Grid ─tap group stack─────▶ Detail (Group Focus at Representative Photo, expandedGroup = group.id)
Detail ─Back───────────────▶ Grid (expandedGroup cleared, scroll position kept)
Grid ─Move Rejects─▶ Confirm sheet ─Move─▶ Grid + toast (Undo / Details)
```

## Groups in detail

- **Two-level model**: Detail operates at either **Timeline** (`expandedGroup == nil`, showing standalone photos and collapsed multi-photo group stacks) or **Group Focus** (`expandedGroup == group.id`, isolating the filmstrip, synchronized zoom, and bounded `←` / `→` swipes to that group's visible members `1 … N`). There is no inline accordion expansion inside the filmstrip and no separate group route/sidebar. Top-bar Back always returns to Grid View in one tap.
- **Representative Photo rule**: a single priority rule governs Grid stack covers, Timeline stack covers, Timeline stack landing photos, and initial Group Focus entry (and collapse snap): `Highest star rating > Non-rejected colour label > First unmarked > First visible`.
- **Read-only protection on collapsed stacks**: when browsing an unexpanded multi-photo group stack in Timeline, marking toolbar controls (dimmed to `0.32` opacity on iPad), vertical stage marking swipes, and marking keys are disabled so you never accidentally rate or batch-reject an entire burst while collapsed; vertical stage drags surface/pulse `"Expand {n} photos"`.
- **"Expand {n} photos"** pill floats centred above the filmstrip without taking its layout height (or at the bottom of the stage in Photo Preview) while on an unexpanded stack in Timeline; tapping it, double-tapping the strip stack, swiping down on the strip stack, or pressing `Return` enters Group Focus without reloading pixels.
- **"Reject {n} unmarked"** pill floats centred above the filmstrip, outside its layout height, in Group Focus once the visible group has both a rated or labeled photo and an unmarked photo. It rejects only visible unmarked photos and displays the transient ~4 s Detail Undo toast (`"Rejected {n} photos"` + **Undo**); tapping **Undo** (or pressing `⌘Z`) restores the whole action and returns to the photo shown when it was tapped. Marking a photo so an active live filter hides it likewise shows a transient ~4 s `"{Action} · hidden by filter"` + **Undo** toast that restores the mark and returns to that photo. Both toasts auto-dismiss after ~4 s or immediately when tapped, when navigating away / leaving Detail, or when replaced by another mark or toast.
- Both pills hide with chrome, and `"Reject {n} unmarked"` also hides when marking is off or no unmarked members remain.
- **Auto-advance in Group Focus**: during first-pass culling (while unmarked visible members remain), a triggering positive mark moves to the next unmarked member, wrapping once; marking the last unmarked member or tapping `"Reject {n} unmarked"` exits Group Focus and advances across Timeline slots (`Next photo` -> next Timeline slot; `Next unmarked photo` -> next unmarked standalone photo or stack with visible unmarked members; `Stay` stays in Group Focus). Clearing a mark stays on the current member without advancing or collapsing, and during second-pass review (entering or editing a group that already had 0 unmarked visible members), marks never auto-collapse or leave the group.
- Zoom and pan stay synchronized within Group Focus; when 100% zoom was entered via focus-region targeting (double-tap `Zoom to focus` hitting a region or tapping a focus region) and has not been manually panned or pinched away, stepping within Group Focus keeps 100% zoom and re-centers onto each incoming photo's preferred focus region (falling back to the previous anchor if a member has no actionable region). Collapsing to Timeline (or switching between Timeline slots) resets zoom to fit if the displayed photo changes or leaves the group (#302, #404).

## Move Rejects

1. Tap Move Rejects. The confirm sheet always opens; nothing moves on the first tap.
2. Optionally, tap the fanned stack to see the rejects in a quick grid, and tap a photo to keep it. The button count updates. A keep is only staged here: nothing is written yet, and tapping again un-keeps.
3. Tap Move. The kept photos have their reject cleared by setting the rating to 0 (written to XMP; the label is untouched), then the rest of the files (RAW, JPEG and XMP together) move to `_Rejected/` (the name is configurable in Settings). Swiping the sheet down instead discards the keeps, and every photo stays rejected.
4. A toast offers Undo, which moves the files back and re-rejects the kept photos, as one step. A partial failure reports how many did not move; Details lists those photos without a retry action. The grid shows the files left on disk.

## Opening a folder

1. The picker returns a folder. The grid appears at once with placeholders.
2. **Reading**: the subtitle reads "412 of 1,284", and the 2 pt progress line fills in `accent`. Thumbnails and existing XMP marks fill in as they are read. The person can already open and mark photos.
3. **Grouping**: the subtitle reads "Finding similar shots…", and the line turns `group`. Stacks form one by one. The photo being viewed never moves, and the grid keeps its scroll anchor.
4. When done, the subtitle returns to "1,284 photos". Move Rejects is hidden until then.
- If Similar groups is off in Settings, stage 2 is skipped.

## Errors

- **Drive disconnected**: drop queued work and pending marks. Show the banner, dim the grid, and show the photos' last-read marks. Marking is off until **Reopen**, which reads the XMP sidecars fresh. An operation already touching a file finishes normally.
- **Lost access** (the security-scoped bookmark is stale): view only from the cache. **Open** re-runs the folder picker.
- **Leaving the folder**: leave immediately without a prompt. Pending writes finish in the background or are dropped when the folder closes.
- **A connected write fails**: show one "Couldn't save the mark." toast, read that photo's marks from the files again and show them. Retain no failed mark and do not retry.
- **Entry**: a folder whose drive is gone shows a faded row with Reconnect, which re-runs the picker for that folder.

## Batch rename (draft, #176)

1. In select mode, tap **Actions ▶ Rename…** in the selection toolbar. The order view shows only the visible selected photos (`batchTargets`); canceling returns to select mode preserving selection, filter, and scroll position.
2. Drag tiles to set the order; the numbers update live. VoiceOver gets "Move earlier", "Move later", "Move to start" and "Move to end". Tap a tile to check focus in a zoomable viewer that swipes in this order. Cancel drops the order.
3. Tap Next. The name sheet previews every new name live. A name already used in the folder warns and is replaced, with the old file set moved to `_Rejected`. Names that can't exist (invalid characters, too long, duplicates in the batch) block Rename.
4. Tap Rename. Pending marks finish first, then the sheet shows progress and can't be dismissed. Afterwards the folder is read again, the mark undo history is cleared, select mode ends, and a toast reports the result. Renaming has no Undo.

## Save to Photos

1. In select mode, tap **Actions ▶ Save to Photos** in the selection toolbar. Clove operates strictly on visible selected photos (`batchTargets`), keeping hidden selected photos (`hiddenSelectedCount`) intact, and resolves at most one existing JPEG or HEIF file per photo (RAW+JPEG prefers JPEG; RAW-only sets are skipped without re-encoding). Disabling happens only when `batchTargets` is empty, the folder location is lost, the folder is changing/closed, or a save is already running; it remains available when marking or rename are gated off.
2. **All eligible**: requests write-only (`.addOnly`) Photos authorization if needed, reads source files inside the folder's security-scoped admitted work, and saves immediately without re-encoding RAW. Selection, filter, and scroll position stay intact, and a bottom glass toast (6 s auto-dismiss, positioned above the batch bar) confirms `"Saved N photos to Photos"`.
3. **Mixed eligible and RAW-only**: presents a confirmation alert (`"Save N photos to Photos?"`, explaining M RAW-only photos have no JPEG or HEIF and will be skipped). Confirming saves the eligible subset and shows `"Saved N photos to Photos · M RAW skipped"`; canceling leaves selection and filter untouched.
4. **All RAW-only**: presents a non-destructive `"No JPEG or HEIF to save"` alert explaining Clove saves existing JPEG and HEIF files without re-encoding RAW, keeping selection and filter intact.
5. **Permission denied / failure**: shows `"Allow Photos access in Settings to save photos."` on add-only permission denial or `"Couldn't save to Photos."` on read/write failure, with no retries or persisted export queue.

## Batch keywords **(proposed)**

1. In select mode, tap **Actions ▶ Add Keywords…** in the selection toolbar. The sheet operates strictly on the visible selected photos (`batchTargets`), noting any `hiddenSelectedCount` when a filter is active.
2. Enter one or more keywords (comma `,`, fullwidth comma `，`, or newline separated, trimmed and deduplicated) and tap **Add** (or press Return) to append them across all visible selected photos while keeping existing per-photo keywords, or tap a folder-wide suggestion chip to append immediately.
3. Tap a chip in **On all selected** to remove a keyword shared by every visible selected photo without affecting keywords unique to individual photos. **Cancel** (or **Done** after chip edits) exits select-sheet presentation preserving selection, filter, and scroll position; **Undo** reverts the last batch keyword edit as one step.

## Theme switching

- Settings ▶ Appearance offers an independent three-way appearance mode (`Light` / `Dark` / `System`, default `System`) and six paired theme families (`Default`, `Catppuccin`, `Darkroom`, `Night`, `Nord`, `Gruvbox`, default `Default`).
- Changing mode preserves the selected family, and changing family preserves the selected mode. In `System` mode, the resolved palette follows the device appearance within the selected family without falling back to `Default`.
- Apply by swapping the resolved token set at the app root (`mode` + `family` + actual device appearance). Photos are never tinted.

## Side-by-side Compare

- **Entry**: Detail toolbar button (`rectangle.split.2x1`) opens Compare in `.stepping` mode (active Group Focus members when expanded, else filtered Detail Timeline; requires `>= 2` photos, defaults `A = current`, `B = next` or previous if last, `activeSide = .challenger`, `isSyncLocked = true`). Grid multi-select batch bar offers `Compare` when **exactly 2** visible photos are selected, opening `.fixedPair` mode (`A = first`, `B = second`, `activeSide = .reference`, `isSyncLocked = true`).
- **Stepping & `Set as A`**: `<` / `>` and filmstrip taps replace B (skipping A), moving the filmstrip's `6 pt` lift and `12 × 4 pt` two-tone marker to the new B and centering B in the Compare filmstrip viewport (naturally clamping at head/tail without half-viewport padding; instant on initial appearance and layout/rotation changes, `CloveMotion.chrome` animated on challenger changes, while manual strip scrolling stays free until B changes); switching `activeSide` updates `A`/`B` badge emphasis without moving the marker off B or re-scrolling. `Set as A` promotes B to A and loads the next available challenger in scope (wrapping forward, or cleanly swapping A and B when scope has exactly 2 photos without modifying marks).
- **Gestures, Focus regions & Sync**: single-finger drag always pans; Detail swipe-to-rate and swipe-to-switch stay disabled. `Focus regions` (`viewfinder`) — in the iPad / iPhone Landscape top bar, Duo Landscape 3-button vertical right rail (`<`, `Focus`, `Sync`), and Duo Portrait native horizontal `.toolbar` (`<` / `Compare` / `[ Focus | Sync ]`) — toggles `showsFocusRegions` live across both panes A & B without disturbing zoom/sync position and preserves state when exiting back to Detail. When `Sync On` (`lock.fill`), pinch, double-tap (preferring eligible focus regions when enabled), and single-finger pan mirror across panes using native-relative scale and normalized center anchors; when `Sync Off` (`lock.open`), panes zoom and pan independently.
- **Marking & Undo**: ratings, Reject, color labels, and hardware keyboard `⌘Z` Undo act strictly on `activeSide` via `BrowsingModel` and never auto-advance or dismiss the Compare pair, even when the active Grid filter would exclude the marked photo. There is no permanent bottom-bar Undo button (`[A/B badge] [Marking Capsule]` with `6 pt` spacing reclaims horizontal space for the filmstrip). Keyboard shortcuts support `Esc` (exit Compare), `←`/`→` (step B in `.stepping` mode), ratings, Reject, labels, and `⌘Z` Undo on the active side.
- **Rotation & folding**: resizing or rotating to portrait iPhone or Duo Cover exits Compare cleanly to Detail on `activeSide`'s photo and restores single-photo full-resolution retention; unfolded Duo Landscape and regular-width Duo Portrait (`669 × 951`) swap cleanly between the 3-button vertical right rail and native horizontal `.toolbar`.
